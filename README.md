# Byte Desktop releases

Byte is a semi-long-haired calico cat that lives on your Mac's desktop: it walks along the bottom of
the screen and the tops of windows, naps, grooms, chases a ball or a butterfly, and can be petted,
picked up and fed from the cat icon in the menu bar.

## Install

1. Download **Byte-Desktop-Installer.pkg** from the [latest release](https://github.com/AdelioSoebroto/byte-desktop-releases/releases/latest).
2. Double-click it. The first time, macOS may say it cannot check the installer: click **Done**, open
   **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, and click **Open Anyway** next to the message about it.
3. Follow the installer. Byte appears straight away and starts by itself at every login.

Works on any Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 11 or later.

## Updates

Byte updates itself: it checks here every few hours and installs a new version while the cat is
resting. Every update is signed, and Byte refuses anything not signed with its release key. Turn it
off with **Update automatically** in the cat's menu, or update at once with **Check for updates…**.
